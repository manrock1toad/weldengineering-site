Heated Automatic Mixing Systems

HAMX-50

  • The HAMX-50 is a heated (control up to 250°F {121°C}) flux hopper/separator.
  • Top hopper has two 25 pound capacity sections, one for recovered flux and one for new flux.
  • Flux from both sections mix together at a preset ratio as the flux is transferred into the the pressure tank.
  • Each section features a high and low level sight window.
  • The HAMX-50 is very useful for sub-arc welding when code requires a specific percentage of new flux to be mixed with recovered flux.
  • Optional MAGSEP-2 separates out magnetic particles from recovered flux.

HPFR-HAMX-3000

  • The HPFR-HAMX-3000 is a heated (control up to 250°F {121°C}) flux hopper/separator and pressure feed system.
  • Top hopper has two 50 pound capacity sections, one for recovered flux and one for new flux.
  • Flux from both sections mix together at a preset ratio as the flux is transferred into the the pressure tank.
  • Each section features a high and low level sight window.
  • The HPFRHAMX-3000 is very useful for sub-arc welding when code requires a specific percentage of new flux to be mixed with recovered flux.
  • Optional MAGSEP-2 separates out magnetic particles from recovered flux.
  • Optional Electronic Flux Level Sensor. A high or low flux level sensor is available to sense flux level of pressure tank or hopper. A loud audible alarm and flashing red light on control panel will signal operator.
  • Heated 100 LB. pressure feed tank. Larger tanks are available.
  • Manufactured with an internal flux cone. This allows complete draining of all flux out of tank. There is no stagnant area in the tank from which flux will not flow out of tank.
