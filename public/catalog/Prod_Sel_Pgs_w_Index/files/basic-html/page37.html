            1.    AV-4 ELECTRIC AIR VALVE. (For all air powered  Flux Vacs.)

                                                The AV-4 electric air valve is for remotely turning air
                                                powered flux recovery vacuums on and off (AP-1,
                                                SUPER-AP-1, AP-1-E/P, SUPER-AP-1-E/P, XP-3, XP-4T,
                                                XP-3TRAC, APT-2, PS-2-A/P, SUPER-PS-2-A/P, and FH-
                                                100-AP etc.).  The AV-4 is installed between the on/off
                                                ball valve of the flux vac, and the air supply.  When
                                                electrical signal is provided to the AV-4, the valve opens,
                                                allowing the flux vac to operate.  Standard input voltage is
                                                115V. (Also available in other voltages.)
                                                See pages 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 12 for typical installa-
                                                tions.
