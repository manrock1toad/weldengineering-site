                                            INDUSTRIAL PRODUCT SELECTION FOR
                                      “MIGHTY-MAC” (HEAVY DUTY) FLUX RECOVERY EQUIPMENT

                                                                 HEAVY CONTINUOUS DUTY VACUUMS

            1A.   MM-1500X (“MIGHTY-MAC 1500X”)

                                                The New MM-1500X (Mighty-Mac 1500X) Heavy Duty Vacuum and
                                                Dust Collector offers great new features.  EXtremely rugged and
                                                durable, eXtremely easy to maintain because large drawer pulls
                                                out to empty flux dust and access filter for easy replacement.
                                                This drawer and all internal covers are simple to remove by means
                                                of four (4) wing nuts. EXtremely safe; micron filters remove
                                                particles below one (1) micron. The air you breath will be much
                                                cleaner.  The MM-1500X also provides auto sensing dust
                                                cleaning logic so filters are always automatically pulse cleaned as
                                                they become dirty (with no operator intervention).  Motor: 2 hp,
                                                TEFC directly coupled to blower.

                                                Input power: 230v/60hz/3ph
                                                Input power: 230v/50hz/1ph

                                                input power: 460v/60hz/3ph
                                                Input power: 220v/50hz/3ph
                                                Input power: 380-415V/50hz/3ph
                                                Input power: 575v/60hz/3ph


            1B.   MM-1500X/P (“MIGHTY-MAC 1500X/P”)

                                                MM-1500X/P (Mighty-Mac 1500X/P) Heavy Duty Vacuum unit only.  Same as
                                                2A above, except that instead of a dust drawer, dust filter  chamber has a
                                                hopper bottom.  This enables continuous dumping of dust to a much larger
                                                dust container below the Mighty-Mac (i.e  FD-55 Dust Drum).  Ideal for very
                                                continuous recovery applications where regular emptying of dust drawer is
                                                inconvenient..

                                                NOTE: Teflon coated polyester filter for extra heavy duty applications.
                                                Factory installed. One required.
