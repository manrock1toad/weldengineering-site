            12.   AFV-32 AUTOMATIC FLUX VALVE.

                                                The AFV-32 is an automated slide valve attachment for
                                                1" I.D. flux feed hose.  Automatically opens or closes flux
                                                flow in 1" I.D. hose, when 115v electrical signal is sup-
                                                plied.  Use with FH-1, HFH-1, FH-100-E/P, HFH-100-E/P,
                                                SUPER-FH-100-A/P-E/P and SUPER-HFH-100-A/P-E/P,
                                                or any gravity feed hopper.  (Typical use would be to
                                                automatically start flux flow when arc-start button is
                                                pushed and to stop flow when weld is finished.) System
                                                includes automated slide valve, air on/off solenoid,
                                                pressure regulator and two five (5) foot lengths of 1/4" I.D.
                                                air hose.

                                                Input power:  115v/1ph/50-60hz and standard shop air
                                                (other voltages available).




                                                AFV-32-1 1/2 is the same as above except it is for 1 1/2”
                                                I.D. flux feed hose.




            13.    FF-3 FLUX FEED PIPE.

                   The FF-3 flux feed pipe enables flux to be fed from a pressure feed tank (PT-”x”00 or other pressure tank is
                   required) to the flux hopper.  Flux flow to hopper will automatically stop when flux level inside hopper is higher
                   than inlet pipe.  Specify flux feed hose size (1/2", 3/4" or 1" I.D. size available).
